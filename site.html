Background & Philosophy

I am Kelwin Cyril J — an Electronics & Communication Engineering student from Trichy, Tamil Nadu, with hands-on experience from internships as a Backend Developer at Infinite Computer Solutions and a Data Analyst at Circulants.

I work across Python, Java, SQL, MongoDB and Generative AI, and I like shipping real systems — hospital management backends, voice-based inquiry tools, inventory platforms and Power BI dashboards.
